English

Thanks for playing World Tree! For questions, suggestions or bugs, email akar.554426@gmail.com with your device model, OS version and app version (bottom of Settings). We usually reply within 3 working days.

FAQ

  • The weather differs from what I see? The game compiles official public data and may lag; in severe weather follow your local weather service.
  • Move my save to a new phone? Settings › Export save, then Settings › Import save on the new device and paste the code.
  • No notifications? Allow notifications in system settings and turn on Settings › Notifications in the game.
  • Restore purchases / cancel? Settings › World Tree Premium › Restore purchases; cancel in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. Refunds are handled by Apple / Google.
  • Delete my data? Turning notifications off deletes server data; for anything else, email us.
