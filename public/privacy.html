繁體中文

本政策說明「世界之樹」（iOS、Android 應用程式及網頁版，下稱「本應用」）會處理哪些資料、用途為何，以及你可以如何控制。本應用不設帳戶，不要求你提供姓名、電郵或電話號碼，我們亦不會出售你的個人資料。

1. 位置

  • 本應用只請求大約位置（Android 為「大概位置」；iOS 為「使用 App 期間」），用於顯示當地天氣、天氣警告及地名。你可以拒絕或隨時在系統設定關閉；拒絕時會改用香港天氣，或使用你在應用內手動選擇的地點。
  • 查詢天氣時，坐標會直接傳送至 Open-Meteo（天氣預報，約小數點後 4 位）及 BigDataCloud（地名，約小數點後 4 位）。香港天文台及澳門地球物理暨氣象局的資料以整份公開資料下載，不會傳送你的位置。
  • 查詢香港、澳門以外地區（例如台灣、日本、美國、加拿大、歐洲）的官方警告時，坐標會四捨五入至小數點後 3 位（約 100 米）傳送至我們的推送伺服器，伺服器只用作即時回答，不會把該坐標連同任何識別碼儲存。伺服器再以概略地區向相關官方機構（例如中央氣象署、日本氣象廳及環境省、美國國家氣象局、加拿大環境及氣候變化部、MeteoAlarm）查詢公開警告。
  • 最近一次位置及天氣結果只儲存在你的裝置上，以便離線時使用。

2. 提醒通知（推送）

只有在你於「設定」開啟提醒通知時才會使用。開啟後，以下資料會傳送至我們位於 Oracle Cloud 的推送伺服器，並與推送權杖一併儲存：

  • 由 Google Firebase Cloud Messaging（FCM）發出的推送權杖（iOS 經 Apple 推送通知服務），平台、應用版本及應用語言；
  • 四捨五入至約 0.5 度及約 0.1 度（約 11 公里）的概略位置、時區，是否位於香港／澳門／台灣，及（在台灣時）縣市及鄉鎮名稱；
  • 簡單遊戲狀態：日期、當日已完成哪些應急行動、樹木是否健在或瀕危、抗風力數值。

這些資料只用於判斷應向你發送哪一種天氣警告及通知內容。關閉提醒通知會即時從伺服器刪除權杖及相關資料；若刪除應用程式，權杖會失效，並於下一次推送時被自動移除。伺服器會暫時於記憶體使用 IP 位址作流量限制，不會作其他用途。

3. 遊戲資料

樹木、成長日誌、圖鑑、設定等全部儲存在你的裝置上（瀏覽器 localStorage／應用程式偏好設定），不會上傳。「匯出存檔」產生的存檔碼只會在你自行複製或分享時離開裝置。刪除應用程式或清除網站資料即會刪除這些資料。

4. 廣告（Google AdMob）

  • 免費版的 iOS 及 Android 應用程式底部會顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告；網頁版不顯示廣告；世界之樹 Premium 用戶不顯示廣告。
  • Google 可能收集裝置識別碼（例如 iOS 的廣告識別碼 IDFA、Android 的廣告 ID）、IP 位址、概略位置、裝置資料及廣告互動資料，用作投放、量度廣告及防止詐騙。詳情請參閱 Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資料。

English

This policy explains what data World Tree (世界之樹; the iOS and Android apps and the web version, “the app”) handles, why, and how you can control it. The app has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number. We do not sell personal data.

1. Location

  • The app asks only for approximate location (Android “approximate”; iOS “While Using the App”) to show local weather, weather warnings and the place name. You can decline or turn it off at any time in system settings; the app then uses Hong Kong weather or a place you choose in the app.
  • For weather, coordinates are sent directly to Open-Meteo (forecasts, about 4 decimal places) and BigDataCloud (place name, about 4 decimal places). Hong Kong Observatory and Macao Meteorological and Geophysical Bureau data are downloaded as whole public feeds; your location is not sent to them.
  • To look up official warnings outside Hong Kong and Macau (for example Taiwan, Japan, the US, Canada and Europe), coordinates rounded to 3 decimal places (about 100 m) are sent to our push server, which uses them only to answer the request and does not store them with any identifier. The server queries public warnings from the relevant agencies (such as Taiwan’s Central Weather Administration, the Japan Meteorological Agency and Ministry of the Environment, the US National Weather Service, Environment and Climate Change Canada and MeteoAlarm) by approximate area.
  • Your latest location and weather results are kept only on your device for offline use.

2. Reminder notifications (push)

Used only if you turn on reminders in Settings. Then the following is sent to our push server (hosted on Oracle Cloud) and stored with your push token:

  • the push token issued by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM; on iOS via Apple Push Notification service), platform, app version and app language;
  • approximate location rounded to about 0.5° and about 0.1° (about 11 km), time zone, whether you are in Hong Kong / Macau / Taiwan, and (in Taiwan) the county and township name;
  • simple game state: the date, which emergency actions you did today, whether the tree is alive or dying, and its wind-resistance value.

This is used only to decide which weather warnings to send you and what the notification says. Turning reminders off deletes the token and this data from the server immediately; if you delete the app, the token stops working and is removed automatically on the next push. The server uses IP addresses briefly in memory for rate limiting only.

3. Game data

Your tree, growth log, field guide and settings are stored on your device only (browser localStorage / app preferences) and are not uploaded. A save code from “Export save” leaves the device only if you copy or share it yourself. Deleting the app or clearing site data deletes this data.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The free iOS and Android apps show a Google AdMob banner at the bottom. The web version shows no ads, and members see no ads.
  • Google may collect device identifiers (such as the iOS advertising identifier IDFA or the Android advertising ID), IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • In the EEA, the UK and some other regions, Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown first. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency asks whether you allow tracking; if you decline, the IDFA is not used and only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change this at any time in system settings.

5. Subscription (World Tree Premium)

  • World Tree Premium is a monthly auto-renewing subscription (HK$8/month in Hong Kong; the store shows the local price elsewhere). Its only benefit is removing ads; tree care, weather warnings and all gameplay are free.
  • Payment is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the subscription; RevenueCat receives a randomly generated anonymous user ID, purchase history and receipts, and basic device and app information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.
  • You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID (App Store account) or Google Play subscription settings.

6. Third-party services

Open-Meteo, BigDataCloud, Hong Kong Observatory, Macao Meteorological and Geophysical Bureau, Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging, AdMob, User Messaging Platform), Apple (push notifications, App Store), RevenueCat, Oracle Cloud (push server hosting) and GitHub Pages (web version hosting, which may keep access logs). Each handles data under its own privacy policy.

7. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information; if you believe a child has given us personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and data deletion

  • Location, notification and tracking permissions can be changed at any time in system settings.
  • Turning reminders off deletes the data on the push server; deleting the app deletes the game data on your device.
  • To ask about, access or delete data related to you (including subscription records held through RevenueCat), email akar.554426@gmail.com, and tell us which device and platform you use so we can identify it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update this page and its effective date.
