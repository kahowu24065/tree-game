World Tree (世界之樹) — Terms of Use

Last updated: 2026-10-01 · Contact: https://github.com/kahowu24065/tree-game/issues

English

World Tree is a game. Weather and warnings come from public weather services and may be late or wrong; always follow official warnings for your safety.

World Tree Premium is an optional monthly auto-renewing subscription (HK$8 per month or the local equivalent shown in the store). It removes ads and adds cosmetic leaf skins and the real-weather album; tree care, warnings and gameplay stay free. Payment is charged to your App Store / Google Play account at confirmation of purchase. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings. Monthly skins can only be collected in their month while subscribed and are usable while the subscription is active.

On iOS, Apple's Standard End User License Agreement also applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
